# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days in Europe and 5 to 8 business days to the US and Canada. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

## Tracking
A tracking number is emailed when the order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Delays
If an order has not arrived 5 business days after the estimated date, support opens a carrier investigation and offers a replacement or refund once the carrier confirms the loss.
