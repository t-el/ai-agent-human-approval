# Refund policy

## Eligibility
Annual and monthly plans can be refunded within 14 days of the charge if the account has not exported more than 500 records. Add-ons and setup fees are non-refundable after onboarding has started.

## How refunds are approved
Support can approve refunds up to $100. Anything above $100 needs a manager's approval. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## What to tell the customer
Confirm the order number, explain that the request has been passed to the billing team, and give the 5 to 10 business day timeline. Never confirm that a refund has been issued until billing has processed it.
