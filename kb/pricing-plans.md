# Plans and pricing

## Plans
Starter is $29 per month for 3 users. Growth is $79 per month for 10 users and includes the HubSpot and Google Sheets integrations. Scale is $199 per month with unlimited users, SSO and priority support.

## Upgrades and downgrades
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Annual plans get two months free.
